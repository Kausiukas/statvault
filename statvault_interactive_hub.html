🚀 Speed & Transhuman Dread Discrepancy Analyzer

Why do Space Marines look slow in real-time strategy games? Canonical lore grants Adeptus Astartes sprint velocities of **50–60 mph (80–100 km/h)**, inducing psychological *Transhuman Dread* in unaugmented mortals. However, real-time strategy engines compress these speeds to **12–15 mph** to preserve tactical micro-management and pathfinding stability.
